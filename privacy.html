🔐 DAWNBREAKER - PRIVACY POLICY Last updated: October 11, 2026 Information processed Depending on the features used, Dawnbreaker may process: Discord user ID Minecraft username provided during an application Selected guild Application status and related moderation records Information necessary to operate party-finding features, such as party membership and scheduled start times Purpose of processing Information is used to: Process and review applications Assign the appropriate Discord roles and nickname Maintain party-finding functionality Record moderation and application decisions Storage and access Information may be stored in the bot's configured persistent storage. Authorized server staff may access application and moderation records when necessary. Information is not sold to third parties. Any third-party infrastructure used to operate the bot may process data as necessary to provide that service. Data retention and deletion Information should only be retained for as long as necessary for the purposes described above. Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their application data by contacting the server's authorized staff or the bot operator. Contact For privacy-related requests, contact the administrator of the Discord server where Dawnbreaker is used or the bot operator. This policy must be updated if the bot's actual data collection, storage, or sharing practices change.
